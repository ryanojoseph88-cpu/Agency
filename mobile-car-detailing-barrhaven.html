FAQ — Barrhaven Mobile Detailing

Do you come to my house or driveway?
Yes — we're fully mobile. We bring all our equipment including water and power, so all we need is access to your vehicle. We serve Barrhaven, Kanata, Nepean, Richmond, Stittsville, and Manotick.
How long does a full detail take?
An interior + exterior detail typically takes 3–4 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Interior-only takes 2–2.5 hours.
What vehicles do you detail?
We detail sedans, 5-seat SUVs, trucks, 7-seat SUVs and minivans, and sprinters/oversized vehicles. Pricing adjusts by vehicle size.
How do I book?
Use the booking form on our website — pick your package, vehicle size, and preferred date and time. We'll confirm within a few hours.
Do you offer recurring detailing?
Yes — many clients book monthly or seasonally. Ask us about repeat-client rates when you book.
