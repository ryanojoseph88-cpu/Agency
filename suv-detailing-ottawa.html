  • Three rows cleaned and vacuumed (where applicable)

Why It Matters

SUVs take more abuse than sedans — more passengers, more gear, more ground clearance that throws grime into wheel wells. We price by vehicle size because the work genuinely takes longer and uses more product. You get a complete detail, not a sedan clean crammed into a larger space.

FAQ

Do you charge more for 7-seat SUVs?
Yes — 7-seat SUVs and minivans have additional seating, a larger cargo area, and more panels to clean. They're priced accordingly.
What about roof rails and sunroofs?
We clean roof rail tracks and sunroof channels as part of our standard exterior service.
How do I book?
Use the booking form on our website — pick your package, vehicle size, and preferred date and time. We'll confirm within a few hours.
Do you offer recurring detailing?
Yes — many clients book monthly or seasonally. Ask us about repeat-client rates when you book.
