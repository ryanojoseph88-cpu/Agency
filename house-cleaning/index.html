Frequently asked questions

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes. We bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer fragrance-free or specific products, tell us when you book.

How much does house cleaning cost in Centrepointe?

Pricing depends on home size, condition and frequency. Send the quote form and we reply with a fixed price, not an hourly guess.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients give us a lockbox or door code. We just need clear entry instructions.

What is the difference between a regular and a deep clean?

A regular clean maintains a home that is already in good shape. A deep clean adds baseboards, inside appliances, detailed bathroom and kitchen scrubbing, and build-up removal. Most first-time clients start with a deep clean.
