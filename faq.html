Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our mobile detailing service.

Do you bring your own water and power?
Yes — we're fully self-contained. We bring our own water supply, pressure washer, generator if needed, and all detailing equipment. You don't need to provide anything except access to the vehicle.
How long does a full interior + exterior detail take?
Typically 3–4 hours for a sedan, and 4–5 hours for a large SUV, van, or truck. The exact time depends on vehicle size and condition.
How long does an interior-only detail take?
2–2.5 hours for a sedan. About 2.5–3 hours for an SUV or truck.
What vehicles do you detail?
Sedans and coupes, 5-seat SUVs and trucks, 7-seat SUVs and minivans, and sprinters/oversized vehicles. Each has its own pricing tier.
Do you detail vans and sprinters?
Yes. Contact us directly for sprinter and oversized vehicle pricing as it varies by size.
What areas of Ottawa do you serve?
We serve Barrhaven, Kanata, Nepean, Stittsville, Richmond, and Manotick. Contact us if you're outside these areas — we may be able to accommodate with a travel fee.
How do I book?
Use the booking form on our homepage. Select your package, vehicle size, and preferred date and time. We'll confirm within a few hours by email and text.
Do you offer same-day or next-day bookings?
We do our best to accommodate short-notice bookings depending on availability. Check the booking calendar for open slots.
What if it rains on my appointment day?
Light rain during an interior service is no issue. For exterior services, we'll contact you to reschedule if there's heavy rain in the forecast. No fees for weather-related reschedules.
Do I need to be home during the service?
No — you just need to leave the vehicle accessible and unlocked (or leave us a key). Many clients go about their day and come back to a clean car.
Are your products pet and child safe?
Yes — we use professional-grade, pH-balanced products that are safe once dry. We don't use harsh solvent cleaners inside vehicles.
Do you offer recurring detailing subscriptions?
Not a formal subscription yet, but many clients book monthly or seasonally. Ask us about repeat-client pricing when you book.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept e-transfer, credit card, and cash. Payment is due on completion of service.
