About Car Detail Nerds

Car Detail Nerds is a locally owned mobile detailing service that has been serving Ottawa, Barrhaven, Kanata, Nepean, Stittsville, Richmond, and Manotick for three years.

We use professional commercial equipment and eco-friendly products — the same tools found in high-end detail shops, brought directly to your driveway. No drop-off, no waiting rooms, no guesswork on pricing.

We've built our reputation on thoroughness, honest pricing, and a simple promise: if you're not happy with any part of the job, we'll fix it on the spot at no charge.
